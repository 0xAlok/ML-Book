## Derivations double-checked

- Soft-margin Lagrangian stationarity: $\partial L/\partial w$, $\partial L/\partial b$, $\partial L/\partial\xi_i = C - \alpha_i - \mu_i$ — matches the TA notes' "$\alpha_i + \beta_i = C$" ($\mu_i$ = their $\beta_i$).
- KKT three-regime proofs in Solutions Problem 3 re-derived from scratch; note the subtle step in (iii): complementary slackness alone gives $\alpha_i^\star \in \{0, C\}$ when margin $< 1$ — primal feasibility ($m_i \ge 1 - \xi_i^\star$) rules out $\alpha_i^\star = 0$ (it would force $\xi_i^\star = 0$ and $m_i \ge 1$). Matches the TA notes' summary.
- Kernel dual: same objective as §32.4 with $K_{ij}$ for ${x^{(i)}}^Tx^{(j)}$ — substitution justified by §24.10 (dual touches data only via inner products).
