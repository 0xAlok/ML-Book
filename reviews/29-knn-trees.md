## Thin / contradictory / beyond-lecture points (flagged, not invented)
1. **Decision trees are in MLT Week 9, not Week 7.** The task said "MLT Week 7 materials … on k-nearest neighbors and decision trees"; the actual tree deck is `Slides - Ashish Tendulkar/Week 9/`. The chapter intro cites Weeks 7 (KNN) and 9 (trees). OUTLINE.md line 29 said "MLT W7" — corrected to "MLT W7, W9" (same precedent as ch28's W6→W3 fix).
2. **"Lazy"/"eager" not in the sources.** The KNN deck says "instance based learning", "no explicit model", "all computations are performed at runtime" — the *concept* is sourced, the *terms* are not. §29.20 uses only the lectures' vocabulary; the omission is stated in §29.20(ii).
3. **Feature importance: not sourced — omitted.** No MLT slide mentions it; the chapter does not present it.
4. **Tie-breaking in KNN votes: not sourced — omitted.** The deck's "majority class" claims assume a strict majority. The chapter's examples keep strict majorities; the one ordering tie (q2's k=5: B2/B4 both d²=9) is between same-label points and cannot change the vote — noted here, not in the chapter.
5. **"Minimum test error" vs the validation discipline.** The KNN slide literally says pick the k with minimum *test* error; the chapter applies §28.10 (select on validation, report on test) per §28.12(ii) and notes the wording difference in §29.7(ii) rather than repeating the slide's phrasing uncritically.
6. **Empty-subset stopping case.** The deck lists it ("no example matched a specific value") without prescribing the leaf label; the chapter states only the case, not the textbook parent-majority convention.
7. **Pruning scope.** Only cost-complexity pruning as the deck gives it (C(T), α by CV); no reduced-error pruning or other schemes — not in the sources.
8. **Complexity claims.** Only the lecture's own: prediction cost logarithmic in training samples (trees); KNN time-consuming at runtime on large training sets. No Big-O training-time claims invented.
9. **The overfitting figure** (tjmachinelearning.com URL in the slides) was NOT reused; `29-tree-depth-overfit.png` is an original schematic, captioned as such.
